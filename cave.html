The Deep Works

Something left machines running down here, shaped like animals, built for work nobody remembers ordering. They are still at it. Bring them down.

Heat is the thing to understand. Every hit you land heats the machine. Fill the bar and it vents — a burst across the floor that hurts — and then it seizes for a turn and takes double damage. You want to force the vent. You just have to not be standing in it.

Cave One needs no account. Deeper caves record your run and pay out.
